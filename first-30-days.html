Day 0 to Day 30.

Every step, mapped before you ever sign — so it never depends on us remembering.

Day 0

You sign, we start the clock

Welcome email within the hour. Your agreement goes out for signature. Behind the scenes, we start moving your phone number and registering your business texting — the two slowest steps — immediately, in parallel with everything else.

Day 1–2

Your number starts moving

We submit your number port and your business texting registration the same day we collect your info. These take the longest of anything in the process, so they never wait behind anything else.

Day 3

Your VA gets the keys

Secure vault access, your tools connected, a baseline captured for where you're starting from, and your welcome packet — your VA's name, photo, working hours, and the one channel to reach them.

Day 5

You meet your VA. Live.

A recorded, three-way kickoff call. We walk through your top 3 tasks together, out loud, so everyone starts from the same page.

Day 7–12

Ramp week: they learn by doing

Week 1, your VA shadows and writes the playbook for your top tasks while watching you work. Week 2, they run those tasks solo and we add a couple more. Nothing gets dumped on you at once.

Day ~10

Your phone goes live

Your port usually lands by now. Same number, same digits — your VA can see it, answer it, and text from it right alongside you.

Phone live
Day 14

We check in — both ways

A checkpoint call with you, and a separate one with your VA. This is the moment we'd catch a bad fit and use the 30-day guarantee, while it's still easy to fix.

Day 25

The playbook is locked in

Your top 5 tasks are documented and filed. A named backup VA has read every one of those SOPs, so a sick day never means a dropped ball.

Day 30

Review, then Active

We show you the before-and-after numbers — missed calls, response time, hours back in your week. Then you're officially active.
